# Human state-space geometry, representation and mechanistic prediction

The fixed clinical DoC/GCS predictor selects observations requiring explanation.
The explanatory analysis examines sensor state-space geometry and its dynamics,
competing effective biological accounts, and predictions for human responses.
These computational operations qualify development evidence. Independent neural
replication and a randomized human experiment require new people and data.

## Independent units and adaptation

People, rather than EEG windows, channel pairs or simulator runs, define the
training, validation, test and uncertainty units. State-space models use fixed
source-wise participant partitions. The genuine clinical human-task copy supplies
the assignments for overlapping tactile people. Data from another source do not
become independent confirmation merely by receiving a development test label.

The released clinical checkpoint remains fixed for discovery. Formal comparisons
between model representations and human geometry use actual hidden activations
of the human-fine-tuned clinical copy, with original input and changed checkpoint
hashes. Fine-tuning must be verified before activation export or comparison.
The detection-trained copy's representation can be investigated in other sources;
that does not make its task labels a validated cross-context consciousness assay.

## Reliable state-space construction

Use genuine, continuous, source-bound EEG from the original ten-second scored
intervals. Signal-only quality decisions retain raw inputs and per-channel masks.
For each source, training observations choose a fixed channel montage. Every
evaluated episode must supply those channels; absent or unqualified channels
produce explicit exclusions, never interpolation. Compare this matched montage
with the recorded variable full-scalp measurements to quantify channel sensitivity.

An explicit early-history channel mean removes nuisance DC offsets using only
samples preceding the prediction target. This conditioning is distinct from
training-person estimates of source scaling, PCA bases and temporal coefficients.
Bounded nonoverlapping bins supply at most400states per episode. Compare PCA and
delay-coordinate state representations, with prespecified rank and ridge grids.
Validation people choose reconstruction/prediction settings before test evaluation.
Episode boundaries are never concatenated into fictitious continuous trajectories.

Assess reconstruction and genuinely recursive future prediction, participant
reliability and conventional/null alternatives. Time shuffle preserves current
channel vectors and covariance; phase controls preserve the specified spectral
structure. Spectra, covariance and clinical-score baselines share evaluation cases.
Use available genuine physiology; an unrecorded pupil/arousal signal stays missing.
Sensor geometry is not automatically cortical anatomy or a nonlinear manifold.

## Model representation to human geometry

Export actual adapted-network hidden activations through scheduled native MATLAB,
using the released preprocessing and exact qualified human signals. Repeated
activations and hidden-layer-to-final-output correspondence qualify extraction.
Bind every output to the adapted checkpoint, original signal and source episode.

Use actual same-person, same-channel, same-layer distances for pairwise prediction
of human state-space differences and original measured outcomes. Training people
fit mappings; validation people select layer and regularization; test people
evaluate them. Preserve original numeric units and nominal report categories.
Report score-only, conventional EEG, human geometry/dynamics, adapted activation
and combined alternatives on identical supported cases, with participant-weight
uncertainty. Missing outcomes, layers and pairs retain explicit dispositions.
An activation distance is not itself neural geometry or experiential distance.

## State-space geometry to competing generators

The bank contains actual, hash-bound native trajectories, configuration parameters
and paired input/control proposals. It exports one cortical population potential.
Fit an effective delay/kernel observation map to multivariate human state traces,
including rotation-invariant temporal geometry and conditional future prediction.
Select observation-map and parameter settings using training and validation people.
Test people do not calibrate the map or choose accounts.

Human states and verified adapted-representation evidence now enter the fitting
objective and diagnostic analysis. This replaces an exclusively five-summary
explanatory loop; the original summary analysis remains a documented reference.
Where actual human response traces are supported, evaluate an effective response
map against genuine paired simulator inputs. Native physical input units, human
stimulus alignment, anatomical mapping and human function require their own
qualification. The map does not manufacture a multiregion biological simulation.

Retain all weak, unsupported, failed, insensitive and equivalent configurations.
Known-generator recovery checks numerical recoverability under specified synthetic
assumptions; they are not recovery of human physiological parameters. Assess joint
training-state support with learned multivariate geometry and other-person support,
instead of accepting candidates from independent coordinate ranges alone.

## Diagnostic search, prospective freeze and human evaluation

Freeze supported computational candidates using training/validation evidence before
opening held-out test outcomes. Record paired native input/control identities,
account predictions, support, uncertainty, ambiguity and failure reasons. A human
diagnostic can remain unqualified when physical or functional mapping is missing.
No preferred result, manifold collapse, effect sign or significance is required.

`manifold_diagnostic freeze` binds the state, representation and generator artifacts
to a prospective protocol. Independent evaluation requires a qualified human assay
and manipulation, registration before outcomes, source-bound measured data, and
people absent from the development registry. The evaluator retains all eligible
randomized assignments, recorded probabilities and original missing outcomes.

The randomized estimator is the equal-person average of trial Horvitz–Thompson
active-minus-control contributions. Participant bootstrap supplies uncertainty.
Missing primary outcomes yield predeclared bounded-outcome identification intervals;
they cannot silently become a complete-case causal point estimate. Hash-bound
data/registration declarations do not themselves certify ethical approval, external
preregistration or the validity of a neural measurement operator.

All cross-system model comparisons retain the human-data fine-tuning prerequisite
and require a qualified common assay. Unrelated tactile, lottery and sleep outcomes
remain different measurements even when their models have all been adapted.
