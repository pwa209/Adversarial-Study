# Existing-data cross-mechanistic extension — frozen before execution

This release asks three bounded questions, developed retrospectively after the earlier DREAM and full-population results. It does not constitute preregistration, independent human replication, or a guarantee of statistical significance. The original behavioral target remains 42, with 26/8/8 participants. Both completed changed-weight human-data fine-tunings and the separate completed clinical human backbone adaptation must pass provenance gates before any branch executes.

1. **Mechanism compensation.** Across the existing 616 zero-input traces, describe effective native-unit and training-common-scaled dynamics. On the actual 48 manipulated configurations, test whether held-configuration factors can be recovered and whether pre-input effective modes predict the existing finite pulse response beyond observed-input and factor-plus-input comparators. Keep variants separate. Group pulse/control and physically duplicate configurations before splitting; estimate scaling and hyperparameters using training and validation only. Published configurations' nominal factor labels are not inversion ground truth. Reuse the completed 664-trace native cache.
2. **Global versus regional human organization.** From the existing raw DREAM N2 windows, test whether fixed posterior sensor delta power adds information beyond global delta and the frozen clinical score. Preserve original participant roles; distinguish experience presence from content recall conditional on experience. Compare frozen held-person Brier predictions and exploratory matched within-person report contrasts. Use the author sensor coordinates, full qualified channel mask, exact original ten-second source window, and a fixed 80% ROI coverage rule. Common-average reference is primary; stored reference, unsaturated-channel and high-frequency-adjusted panels are fixed sensitivities. Report all exclusions and actual eligible denominators. This public-cohort spatial association is not an independent replication or deep-source localization.
3. **Internal pathway balance.** Reuse the existing 48 matched pulse/control pairs plus the union of 185 human-training/validation-supported zero-input traces. Parse at most 281 original native files. Verify their hashes, unchanged clock, cached V/Q/noise and all 41 saved dendritic voltage contributions against the source-defined nine population sums, including the simulator's actual printing precision. Quantify raw-unit absolute and net pathway mass, cancellation, response energy and timing. Separate tonic balance from mean-centered fluctuations. Use shared physical scales, never componentwise near-zero baseline standard deviations. Preserve noise/variant/input matching, conventional matches and human-fit support; do not force unsupported matched pairs.

## Fixed uncertainty and testing policy

- Seed: 20261024. Bootstrap repetitions: 2,000. No additional draws, alternative seeds or optional stopping based on a p-value.
- Human held predictions are first frozen, then resampled by participant. Matched contrasts are reduced to participant means before resampling/sign flips. These are conditional intervals, not uncertainty from rerunning model development or new participant recruitment. Episodes, channels, time bins and bootstrap draws never increase independent human N.
- Four primary human comparisons form one Holm family: two held predictive increments and two matched report contrasts. Ten fixed sensitivity comparisons form a separately labeled Holm family. Untestable comparisons stay in the planned denominator with p=1. Report effects, intervals, uncorrected and corrected p-values, counts and null results.
- Configuration bootstrap uses paired physical configuration units within family and implementation. It measures stability within this deliberately sampled finite simulator bank, not a human population or a random sample of all possible biological systems. No human significance claims or pathway p-values derive from simulated configurations.
- A legitimate null, missing matched support or insufficient outcome variation is a reportable disposition. Qualification tests technical integrity and provenance; it does not require a favored effect direction or significance.

## Scope and execution

The native model contains nine effective populations at one spatial node. Existing pulses are external-input perturbations in the patched variant, not selective circuit interventions, an anatomical whole-brain connectome, or within-configuration dose sweeps. These branches can describe compensation and expose parameter ambiguity; they cannot establish a causal mechanism of human consciousness.

No retraining, new simulation, download, restoration, compaction or completed numerical analysis is repeated. Three independent child processes run inside a single 16-CPU, 64-GiB, 24-hour Slurm allocation, with four BLAS threads each. A 4-CPU, 8-GiB, two-hour qualification runs first; the analysis uses afterok dependency and the unchanged full original storage guard. Outputs are aggregate tables/JSON in a new immutable result namespace. Source and all input metadata are hash-pinned, and each actual raw source is reverified. A failed branch preserves completed sibling output.

The module-specific methods files supply the exact estimands, features, matching rules, equations and output definitions. This plan and all executable sources are sealed together before upload and execution.
