## Execution and inference retained

Centaur and its matching base comparator receive the same training people,
human observations, original rank-8 LoRA architecture and native update budget
(two epochs, at most 64 optimizer updates). Whole-person grouping, exposure
exclusion, eight validation people, eight test people and the seed stay fixed.
Changing the current target does not authorize outcome-driven reassignment or
selection of additional people from another task.

**Both genuine native human-data fine-tunings must complete and their changed
adapter artifacts must verify before held-person test evaluation and comparison.**
Prepared data, software tests and queued jobs do not establish completed
fine-tuning. The clinical discovery instrument remains frozen; a claim comparing
neural and behavioural systems additionally requires the qualified common human
assay and observation bridge specified in the [protocol](Original_Study_Protocol.md).

The 42-person target is met only by an actual source-verified preparation receipt
containing all 42 disjoint assigned identities. Training, evaluation and model
comparison retain their own completion criteria. Queue and execution status are
reported separately in [Deployment status](Deployment_Status.md).

With eight independent test people, repeated trials do not increase the number
of independent participants. The original 48-person plan's power or precision
claims do not carry over. This remains a computational behavioural task
comparison; it does not itself establish a biological mechanism, independent
human replication or a randomized human experiment.
