OBSERVATION CONSTRAINTS ON MECHANISTIC INFERENCE
Figure set: five main figures and seven supplementary figures

General reporting conventions
All figures use completed, hash-verified aggregate outputs. These plots introduce no new fitted models, significance tests or bootstrap runs. Configuration counts are not human sample sizes. Model-bank percentile ranges describe a deliberately sampled finite bank conditional on the existing trained fits; they are not confidence intervals for human physiology. All analyses are retrospective development. The main figures concern a neural-field model with one spatial node and nine effective neural populations. They establish neither an anatomical whole-brain connectome nor a selective human circuit mechanism. White backgrounds and black text are used throughout; the supplied colours encode graphical marks. Small deterministic offsets expose coincident points without changing their data values.

The held set contains ten physical configurations: seven combined and one each gain, coupling and history. Singleton strata remain fixed in this resampling. (b) Paired cortical and full-population errors for those same ten configurations. Lines connect configurations; diamonds show means. (c-e) Observed and predicted log gain, coupling and history multipliers. Open brown circles are cortical predictions and filled red circles are population predictions; dashed lines indicate equality. Panel (a) does not provide a direct confidence interval for their paired difference; no new paired significance claim is introduced by this figure.

Figure 5 | Raw-unit pathway contributions and the coverage of matched comparisons.
The saved dendritic voltages are already weighted and filtered postsynaptic contributions; no second coupling multiplication is applied. Pulse quantities use matched pulse-minus-control voltages with verified common noise, baseline, configuration and clock. (a) Net versus absolute pathway voltage mass in 48 pulse configurations x nine populations, all 432 of which have positive masses. For contributions D_j, absolute mass is integral(sum_j abs(D_j)) and net mass is integral(abs(sum_j D_j)). The equality line represents no cancellation. (b) Pulse voltage cancellation, 1 - net mass/absolute mass, by population across the same 48 configurations. (c) Cancellation after removing each pathway's temporal mean in the 185 human-training/validation-supported zero-input configurations. These pool the two named implementations descriptively; fit support is not physiological equivalence. In (b,c), boxes show medians and quartiles, whiskers extend to 1.5 interquartile ranges, and all finite individual configuration values are overlaid. Repeated populations are not independent configurations. (d) Population 1 cancellation versus the mean over populations 2-9 in the pulse configurations; the dashed line indicates equality. (e) Existing matched-pair coverage after score, conventional-feature and human-fit-support requirements: zero-input comparisons 1,014 -> 4 -> 4; pulse comparisons 168 -> 1 -> 0. The count axis uses log10(count + 1), with ticks labelled by counts. Comparisons can share configurations and are not independent trials. (f) Raw RMS pathway-signature versus population-signature differences in mV for all 1,187 score-matched comparisons; the five conventional-feature-matched comparisons are highlighted. These are physical-unit differences, not per-component baseline-SD-normalized effects. No configuration-based p-values or human causal inference are asserted.

Sources: pathway_balance/population_pathway_balance.csv; configuration_summary.csv; matched_pathway_contrasts.csv; source_equation_proof.json; summary.json.

Figure S5 | Human spatial information: held-person and within-person contrasts.
(a,b) Person-mean Brier losses for the frozen baseline (clinical score plus global delta power) and its posterior-delta augmentation, separately for experience presence and content recall conditional on experience. Lines connect the same six original test participants. The experience contrast uses 41 episodes; the recall contrast uses 29. No test participant is moved into training or validation. (c,d) Exploratory within-person matched log10(posterior/global delta power) differences for experience and recall, respectively. The experience contrast comprises 15 matched episode pairs from 13 people; the recall contrast comprises seven pairs from six people. Each plotted value is a participant mean. Zero reference lines are shown. These are development-cohort associations, not independent replications or evidence that clinical GCS predictions are awareness probabilities. All four primary Holm-adjusted p-values are approximately 0.946. The existing human uncertainty calculations used 10,000 participant bootstrap draws; no bootstrap is recomputed for plotting.

Sources: human_spatial/held_person_predictions.csv; matched_pairs.csv; summary.json; plot_data/human_person_effects.csv.

Uncompleted inferential work
No matched-feature/capacity recovery analysis, independent-noise replication or second-model-family validation is represented as completed. No new independent human mechanism or randomized human intervention is claimed. A manuscript using this set must retain these distinctions and identify all retrospective/exploratory analyses.
