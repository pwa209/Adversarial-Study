# Global versus posterior sensor organisation in DREAM

The question is whether posterior sensor delta power retains report information beyond the frozen clinical score and global delta power. This uses existing public development observations, not new recruitment or independent replication. Reported experience, experience without content recall and reported no experience retain their source meanings. A posterior report association may reproduce an original finding in this same Tononi cohort; it is not automatically a new anatomical mechanism.

## Actual samples and fixed spatial operators

The six metadata roles are `dream_observations`, `dream_trajectories`, `state_split`, `state_receipt`, `prepared_recordings`, and `electrode_coordinates`, each a `{path, sha256}` record, optionally under `files`. Every metadata file is checked before and after analysis. The original completed state receipt must bind the prepared recording inventory and each recording's raw SHA256. Scored and trajectory episode identities, scores, labels and exact time bounds must agree. Each actual source NPZ is hash checked before and after reading. Only source N2 ten-second windows with the original actual multichannel quality mask are used; the 32-channel PCA montage is not substituted for this full sensor mask. Exact acquisition timing, units, continuity and original boundaries are checked. No signals, missing channels or anatomical locations are imputed.

The author HydroCel GSN 256 coordinate file defines geometric **sensor** ROIs, before any outcomes are fitted. Its EGI Cartesian coordinates are in centimetres, +Y anterior and +Z superior. Global upper scalp is `z>=0`; posterior is `y<=-3` and `z>=0`; frontal is `y>=3` and `z>=0`. The author's final `Cz` coordinate corresponds to source `Chan 257`. At least 80% of each fixed ROI must be present in the original qualified-channel mask, excluding any source bad channels; otherwise the episode is excluded with a reason. Actual channel identities/counts remain in the feature table so missing-channel changes are visible. These ROIs are not cortical source localization or measurements of thalamic/basal-ganglia activity.

The primary reference subtracts the instantaneous mean of the qualified global ROI. The stored source reference is a fixed sensitivity. No claim is made that its originally unreported reference is known. Both references must be technically measurable for an episode to enter either comparison. Two-second Hann Welch windows, 50% overlap, segment mean removal and actual source sampling frequency give channel power in microvolts squared. Fixed bands are 1–4 Hz delta and 20–40 Hz high frequency; global/posterior/frontal power is the arithmetic channel mean before log10. The primary spatial predictor is `log10(posterior delta/global delta)`. Frontal power and posterior/frontal contrasts are exported descriptively, without selecting a favorable ROI or adding a separate inferential scan.

## Two report constructs and two complementary comparisons

Experience presence contrasts experience with or without content recall against no reported experience. Content recall conditional on experience contrasts recalled experience with experience without recalled content and excludes reported no experience. Each binary construct keeps its own cases and denominators.

For held-person incremental prediction, the original state participant roles are preserved exactly. The actually retained test sample is reported rather than forced to five people; six original role assignments may yield fewer eligible people after qualified source restrictions. Baseline predictors are the frozen clinical score and global log delta. The spatial model adds posterior-minus-global log delta. Person-balanced training estimates scaling and regularized logistic coefficients. Only the original validation people choose ridge from `.01, .1, 1, 10` using equal-person Brier loss, with ascending-ridge tie breaking. The original test labels cannot choose sensors, scaling, ridge or coefficients. Both models score identical test episodes, and their within-person mean Brier differences are averaged equally across people. A negative augmented-minus-baseline difference favors spatial information. Missing training classes, validation cases or test cases produce an explicit unqualified/insufficient result, not a replacement split.

The complementary retrospective comparison uses all eligible existing people. It matches opposite report classes only within the same source, person and N2 stage, requiring absolute score difference <=.05 and global log10(delta) difference <=.1 (a power ratio of about1.26). Deterministic ascending normalized score/global distance and episode-ID ties create disjoint greedy pairs. Posterior power never selects or ranks a pair. Pair posterior-relative-delta differences are first averaged within person, then equally across people. The algorithm is not an optimal matching estimator and does not imply conditional random assignment. No caliper is widened if few people remain.

## Uncertainty, fixed sensitivities and inferential limits

Ten thousand bootstrap draws resample whole participant contrasts. For prediction they condition on the already fitted training/validation models; they do not include model-training uncertainty. For matched comparisons they condition on the fixed features and matching. They cannot increase the number of people or manufacture significance. Point estimates, intervals, actual independent-person counts, exact/Monte Carlo sign-flip references and leave-one-person-out estimates are retained regardless of direction. Sign flips require independent people and a symmetric-zero participant contrast, and are not a randomized-intervention test. With five people the smallest possible exact two-sided value is .0625, even if all five effects share a sign.

The four primary tests comprise two held-person prediction increments and two retrospective matched report contrasts at common average reference. They receive one Holm family. Ten prespecified sensitivities form a separate Holm family: two prediction and two matched tests using the stored reference, two prediction and two matched tests excluding any saturated episode, and two prediction tests adding global and posterior-relative 20–40 Hz power to **both** baseline and spatial models. The high-frequency nuisance control reduces one measured artifact alternative; it does not establish absence of muscle or other artifacts. Declared but untestable contrasts remain in the multiplicity family as p=1 for its correction; no favorable panel or result is promoted after inspection.

Outputs are aggregate episode features/dispositions, test predictions, matched pairs, fitted-model provenance, summary and receipt. `qualification_probe(inputs)` verifies one actual qualifying source window and both ROI/reference operators without fitting a report effect or gating on statistical direction. `run(inputs, output, seed=20261024)` requires a new output directory. The caller enforces target42 (26/8/8), both behavioral human-data fine-tunings before cross-system comparison and the separate clinical adaptation provenance gate. This module does not repeat training, restoration, compaction or previously completed analyses.
